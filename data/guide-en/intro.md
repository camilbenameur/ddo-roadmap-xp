# XP Roadmap: level 1 to the cap, then the past life loop

This is the roadmap from level 1 to 36 for a character that reincarnates. A reincarnation restarts your character on a new life (TR stands for True Reincarnation). Reopen this guide at the start of each new life, alongside your build, and start at chapter 0.

It is for a character that already has lives behind it. It does not cover a character's very first life, whose XP and constraints do not apply here.
