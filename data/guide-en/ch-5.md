## 5. Heroic 15 to 20 (also the Iconic route)

Route source: the "Level 15 to 20" thread on forums.ddo.com, 3 pages, 47 posts, read in full [F15]. Quest levels come from the DDO Compendium. Roll Call, Times Long Past and The Lords of Dust were edited after U81, so their level may have changed [R89] [R75] [R97].

Stay at most 2 levels above a quest's base level to keep the full delving bonus. Above that, it is halved for the whole group [W-XP].

### 5.3 XP banking and sagas

- Bank XP and sagas to shorten 18 to 20 [F15].
- Turn in your sagas between levels 18 and 20 [F15]. Keep them unturned only if a Lesser or Epic Reincarnation follows. Before a True or Racial Reincarnation, turn them in. The turn-in rules are in §6.2.
- Drink an XP elixir before you turn in a saga, since it applies to the reward (wiki, not cross-checked) [R217].

Useful sagas, XP at Normal / Hard / Elite [C-SG]:
- Heroic Masterminds of Sharn (15-16) and Heroic Cogs (15-16): 16,000 / 22,000 / 35,000.
- Honor of the Huntsilver (16-19): 26,000 / 36,000 / 56,000.
- **Perils of Cormyr (15-19)**: 38,000 / 53,000 / 83,000.
- Heroic Vecna Unleashed (level 18 quests): 21,000 / 29,000 / 45,000.
