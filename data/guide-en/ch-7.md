## 7. Legendary 30 → 36 and the jump to the cap

**Do this from level 30:** play only Legendary quests with a Legendary base level of 30 or higher (table in §7.2). Epic quests give no Legendary XP, even an Epic level 30 quest like The Mask of Deception. Run them in Reaper [W-RD]. The only free quests found are Temple of Elemental Evil Part One (30) and the three Lordsmarch quests (34): Frame Work, Diplomatic Impunity and Eyes of Stone [R110] [R63].

### 7.1 Basic rules

- Level 36 takes **12,600,000 Legendary XP**. Milestones: 31 at 1.6M, 32 at 3.6M, 33 at 5.7M, 34 at 7.9M, 35 at 10.2M [W-LL].
- **Legendary XP is never lost.** If you drop below 30 (True, Iconic or Epic Reincarnation), you get every Legendary level back when you return to 30 [W-LL] [W-XP]. The full Legendary life therefore happens once per character. After that, every life jumps to the highest level already reached. Axel ("Axel's DDO Channel" on YouTube) calls this the 30 → cap jump [AX].
- There is no Legendary Reincarnation yet [W-LL].
- Tomes of Learning do not apply to Reaper XP or XP stones, and the Epic Tome stops working when you take level 30 (wiki, not cross-checked) [R214].
- There has been no Reaper XP in the wilderness since U81 [N81] [R113].
