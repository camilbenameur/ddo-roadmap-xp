## 3. A single route, several entry points

The route runs from level 1 to level 36 and is the same for every reincarnation type. Each life type joins it at its starting level.

| Reincarnation | You join the route at | Past life gained |
|---|---|---|
| Heroic TR | level 1, then [chapter 4](/guide/4/) to [chapter 8](/guide/8/) | dominant class [WR] |
| Racial TR | level 1, then [chapter 4](/guide/4/) to [chapter 8](/guide/8/) | the race you leave [WR] |
| Epic Reincarnation (at level 30) | level 20, then [chapter 6](/guide/6/) to [chapter 8](/guide/8/) | 1 Epic Past Life [WR] |
| Iconic TR (at level 30) | level 15, then [chapter 5](/guide/5/) to [chapter 8](/guide/8/) | iconic + dominant class [WR] [R218] |

- Once a character has earned its legendary XP, 30 to 36 is immediate on every later life ([chapter 7](/guide/7/)). Do the life's Legendary quests on Reaper before you reincarnate (golden rule 7).
- A True Reincarnation can be done from level 20 [WR]. Axel (Axel's DDO Channel on YouTube) describes racial lives as 1-20 lives [AX].
- Epic Reincarnation restarts at level 20 [WR] [N81] [UNVERIFIED]. Check it in game.
- The order of the reincarnations is covered in [chapter 9](/guide/9/).

### 3.1 Iconic rules (wiki, not cross-checked) [R218]

- Iconic TR is only possible from an Iconic life, at level 30. Heroic TR is not available during an Iconic life.
- Epic Reincarnation is open to Iconics.
- Each Iconic starts with a fixed class level. Examples: Purple Dragon Knight = Fighter, Tiefling Scoundrel = Bard.
- Iconics are probably paid content (inferred, [UNVERIFIED]: no verified claim confirms it).

### 3.2 XP to earn per bracket

| Bracket | XP | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | 1,900,000 on lives 2 to 4 (same as a first life), 2,850,000 on lives 5 to 7, 3,800,000 from life 8 on. The new curve applies at your next True, Racial or Iconic Reincarnation. | [N72] [R205] |
| Iconic start | 1,050,000 banked XP, enough for level 15. You are 850,000 XP short of level 20 (calculation based on [N72]). | [W-IH] [R218] |
| 20 to 30 | 8,250,000 in total, or 825,000 per epic level (level 25 = 4,125,000). The U81 notes publish no XP table. [UNVERIFIED after U81] | [R111] [R219] [R113] |
| 30 to 36 | 12,600,000 legendary XP ([chapter 7](/guide/7/)) | [W-LL] |

If you decline the Iconic tutorial, you start at level 1 and gain no quest XP until you take level 14, because of the banked XP cap [R218].
