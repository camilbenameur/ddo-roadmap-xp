## 1. The 8 golden rules

1. **Run each quest once per life, on the highest difficulty you can handle.** Each repeat costs -20% XP (§2.1) [W-XP].
2. **Do not over-level.** Stay within 2 levels of the quest in Heroic, and within 4 in Epic and Legendary (§2.3) [W-XP].
3. **At level 30+, only Legendary quests give XP.** Heroic and Epic quests give 0 (§7) (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-XP] [W-LL] [R220].
4. **Not all boosts stack.** One equipped XP item and one elixir count (§2.5) [R179] [R186].
5. **Bank your levels.** Keep your Heroic sagas unturned only if a Lesser or Epic Reincarnation follows. Before a True or Racial Reincarnation, turn them in (§6.2) [W-SG].
6. **Drink an XP elixir before the big saga turn-ins.** Elixirs apply to the saga reward (wiki, not cross-checked) [R217].
7. **Do the Legendary quests on Reaper before you True Reincarnate.** The first Reaper run gives +200% Reaper XP, and a True Reincarnation resets it (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-RD] [R220] [AX] (§8.1).
8. **Do not True Reincarnate from the cap before the life's Legendary quests are done.**
