## 4. Heroic 1 → 15

- Open each quest once on Elite or Reaper (VIP, or 2 reincarnations). The first run gives +45% and up to +150% delving bonus [W-XP].
- Stay at most 2 levels above the quest's base level to keep the full delving bonus [W-XP].
- Fill the heroic sagas in §4.4. Do not turn them in if a Lesser or Epic Reincarnation follows, and turn them in before a True or Racial Reincarnation (golden rule 4) [W-SG] [R01] [SG].

Reliability: medium for quest names and levels, low for order and speed. The sources predate U81.

### 4.1 Approach by access

- **Elite and Reaper access** (wiki, not cross-checked): a quest opens on Reaper for any character who can open Elite. A VIP always can. An F2P or Premium character in heroic must have finished the quest on Hard, or have 2 reincarnations, or play in a group with someone who can open it (see §2.3). You cannot enter more than 4 levels above the quest's Normal level in heroic [R220] [W-RD].
- **From the 3rd life, or with VIP**: vary quests on Elite and Reaper instead of redoing Normal or Hard [W-XP].
- **F2P or Premium, first two lives only**: the [SG] series replays each free quest on Normal, Hard, then Elite to stack first-time bonuses, with 18 h pauses, because the ransack repeat penalty is halved every 18 hours [SG].
- **Leveling on R1** is the most common use, for the Reaper XP bonus and to spend Reaper Points already earned, according to Axel's DDO Channel on YouTube [AX]. It also gives the full 150% delving bonus on the first run [W-XP].
- **Quests with no Reaper** (wiki, not cross-checked): all Korthos quests, the solo quests (Arachnophobia, The Miller's Debt, The Grotto, Hall of the Mark), Tangleroot Gorge (levels 3-7), the Challenges, Protect Baudry's Interests and Stop Hazadill's Shipment (level 2). Later: Mask of Deception and Precious Cargo, with a conflict on Mask of Deception explained in ch. 6 "Things to watch" [R220].
- **XP to level 20**, per the VIP route guide in §4.2: 1.9M (lives 1-4), 2.85M (lives 5-7), 3.8M (lives 8+) [R01] [N72].

### 4.2 Route by level range

Pick your column and play each row in order. Move on when you reach the next range. If you run short on XP, replay quests on Elite [R01] [SG].
