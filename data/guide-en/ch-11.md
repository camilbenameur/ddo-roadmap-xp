## 11. Uncertainties

Every open point of this guide is parked here. Open a line to see what is known and what is missing.

<details><summary>Levels 1-15 route after U81 - open</summary>

Known: Vooduspyce, author of a level 1-20 quest guide on the High Lords forum (last updated Aug 2025, before U81), writes for Reaper with a 20% potion, VIP and Monster Manual [R01]. Ina's guide dates from 2019 [R02]. They disagree on the start (Korthos or Keep on the Borderlands) [R01] [R02].

Missing: a 2026 VIP or Premium route, and a ranking by XP per minute.

</details>

<details><summary>XP changes and leveling speed - unverified</summary>

Known: the U81, U81.4, U82 and U82.1 notes cite no XP change [R113] [R155] [R156]. The thread on martial past lives is about AC, not XP [R210].

Missing: the thread "The State of Leveling in DDO Is Getting Ridiculous" could not be read.

</details>

<details><summary>XP per minute - unverified</summary>

Known: the Compendium gives a duration category (Short to Very Long), with no minutes [R115]. The only benchmarks in minutes are player opinions from before U81 [R212]. One wiki user page (Zhildye) counts +6% group bonus per other player in an epic Reaper party, which does not match the +1% per member in the table (wiki, not cross-checked) [R219].

Missing: durations measured by players since U81. Only Reaper XP per skull can be compared.

</details>

<details><summary>Epic XP 20 to 30 - unverified</summary>

Known: 8,250,000 XP, a 2015 developer announcement that was relayed [R111]. A wiki user page repeats it (wiki, not cross-checked) [R219]. The U81 notes publish no XP table [R113].

Missing: an XP table verified after U81.

</details>

<details><summary>Quest levels and names - open</summary>

Known: most Compendium pages predate U81.

Missing: Grim and Barrett has no quest page, and U81 only mentions it in a fix [R113]. Vale of Twilight, Morgrave and the Eveningstar quests are not verified. For Secret of the Storm Horns, the level is read (heroic 19, epic 27), but the XP and the exact pack are missing.

</details>

<details><summary>Reaper type thresholds and damage - disputed</summary>

Known: the Compendium (an older page) places Despair, Doom and Vengeance at 5 skulls and up [R151]. Axel's DDO Channel (YouTube) places Vengeance at R5 and Doom at R7 [R152]. The Doom Reaper page lists no location [R153]. U81.4 changed the damage reduction per skull (raids: 77% at R1 down to 23% at R10; quests: skulls 7 to 10) [R155].

Missing: an in-game observation or an up-to-date page, and the full damage table after U81.4. Until then, expect Doom from R5.

</details>

<details><summary>Commendations of Valor at Legendary - disputed</summary>

Known: the Commendation of Valor page describes epic quests only [R162]. The Heart Seed page cites "any level 20+ quest once at level 30" and does not mention Legendary [R163]. Players contradict each other: Fhrek (2 Heart Seeds at cap), Dragavon (epic or Legendary), JustHavingFunBro (Legendary only) [R164]. The wiki multiplier, quest level - 17, is an epic-only formula, weak against the 4,200 of a heart (inferred) (wiki, not cross-checked) [R215].

Missing: an in-game test or a reply from the developers.

</details>

<details><summary>Tokens of the Twelve - unverified</summary>

Known: the Compendium gives no quantity per chest and no drop rate [R158]. The wiki gives rough averages of 1 to 2.5 tokens per quest on Epic Elite, partly outdated (wiki, not cross-checked) [R216].

Missing: a current, verified rate.

</details>

<details><summary>Elixirs, Tomes, shrines, XP items - unverified</summary>

Known: only the wiki says that elixirs queue up, strongest first [R213], and that Tomes persist through a TR [R214] (wiki, not cross-checked). The Compendium is silent on stacking [R175]. VIP Sovereign III lasts 12 h (infobox) or 3 h (description) [R178]. Shrine of Experience I needs guild level 13 or 10 depending on the page [R186]. That equippable XP items do not stack comes from the text of their pages, with no in-game test [R184].

Missing: an in-game test.

</details>

<details><summary>Iconic lives - partly unverified</summary>

Known: an Iconic life starts at level 15, with 1,050,000 XP banked (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-IH] [R218]. The Iconic True Heart of Wood price (1,495 DDO Points or 4,200 Commendations) is confirmed.

Missing: entering an Iconic life from a non-Iconic life with that heart, the restart level (20) after an Epic Reincarnation, and the starting XP on life 2 and later [R205].

</details>

<details><summary>Legendary sagas - open</summary>

Known: for Isle of Dread, Vecna Unleashed and Myth Drannor, the True Elite value looks wrong, probably a page error [C-SG]. The Compendium lists 14 entries for Terror of Demogorgon, including 13 heroic level 11 quests and Terror of the Demon Lords, so the figure of 12 quests is not confirmed [R116] [R122]. Seven Demogorgon quests were not read. The reward bug reported at U81 was not rechecked [C-SG].

Missing: up-to-date saga pages.

</details>

<details><summary>Legendary quests to check - open</summary>

Known: the legendary quest list was read quest by quest. Several Myth Drannor quests (Book Burning, Safehold, Ettercap Incursion, Portal to Below, Seeds of Decay, Oghma's Song, Secrets of the Red Wizards) and the other Isle of Dread, Fables, Mists and Saltmarsh quests were not read. No free quest at levels 35 to 37 was found; the only free ones noted are Temple of Elemental Evil Part One (30) and the three quests at 34 [R110] [R63]. No 2025 or 2026 player statement on the fastest legendary quests was found.

Missing: durations in minutes of the legendary quests, the legendary levels of the other Mists quests (31 or 32) [R148], and the XP and Reaper XP of the Court of Strahd event.

</details>

<details><summary>Reaper XP formula - disputed</summary>

Known: the wiki gives duration multipliers of 0.8, 1, 1.2 and 1.4 (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220]. The Compendium values at level 30 and up fit 2 x (50 + 3 x level) for a Medium quest, plus or minus 1.2 x level per duration step; below level 30 there is no x2 and the step is 0.6 x level (calculated) [R114].

Missing: which of the two describes the current game.

</details>

<details><summary>Martial past life passive since U81.4 - disputed</summary>

Known: the official effect is +1% AC, +1 MRR and +1 MRR cap per stack [R155]. Players disagree on whether the stacks multiply or add [R196]. A thread describes the mechanism as a damage-reduction type effect [R210].

Missing: a calculation from the developers or a numbers test.

</details>

<details><summary>Order of the reincarnations - open</summary>

Known: the dated opinions come mostly from 2023 and 2024. The November 2026 threads are about when to take racial action points [R199] [R200].

Missing: a 2026 opinion against Axel's cycle was not found [AX], and the developers have made no decision on racial action points.

</details>
