## 8. Reaper XP

Reaper XP (RXP) comes only from Reaper difficulty. At level 30+, run the Legendary quests with the most RXP per skull (list in chapter 7) in a party at R4 or higher. Spend your first points on Grim Barricade, the defensive Reaper tree [AX] [R220] [W-RE]. The skull advice comes from Axel (Axel's DDO Channel on YouTube) [AX].

### 8.1 The rules

- RXP of a quest = (50 + 3 × quest level) × number of skulls, doubled if the base level is 30 or higher (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220].
- Your first Reaper run of a quest in a life gives × 3 (wiki, not cross-checked). A True Reincarnation resets it, so vary your quests [R220].
- No RXP in the wilderness since U81 [N81]. Under 10 kills you earn 0 RXP (wiki, not cross-checked; the test value may be old) [R220].
- At level 30+, only Legendary quests (base level 30+) give RXP [R220].
- Earned RXP is kept on reincarnation, but you must reallocate your points [R220].

<details><summary>How the numbers are built</summary>

- The Compendium gives RXP per skull by duration category (Short, Medium, Long, Very Long), with no durations in minutes [R115].
- **Medium** quest at level 30+ = 2 × (50 + 3 × level). Below level 30 there is no × 2 (calculation on [R114]).
- Each duration step adds or removes 1.2 × level at level 30+ (0.6 × level below 30): Short = -1, Medium = 0, Long = +1, Very Long = +2 (calculation) [R114].
- Checked on five values at level 30+: level 37 Very Long = 410.8 [R122]; level 36 Very Long = 402.4 [R114]; level 36 Long = 359.2 [R127]; level 34 Long = 344.8 [R142]; level 32 Short = 253.6 [R146].
- Checked on lower-level quests too: The Druid's Curse level 17 Long = 111.2 and level 23 Long = 132.8 [R211]; The Vault of Night level 10 Very Long = 92 [R05]; Impossible Demands epic 21 Medium = 113 [R103]; A Legend Revisited epic 25 Long = 140 [R108]. The Mask of Deception, epic 30 Medium = 280, shows the × 2 starting at level 30 [R94].
- The wiki gives duration multipliers of × 0.8 / 1 / 1.2 / 1.4 (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220]. They would give 379.2 for a level 36 Long quest, against the 359.2 recorded, so use the Compendium formula (calculation, [R220] versus [R114]).
- Example, level 36 Long quest: Medium = 2 × 158 = 316. Long = 316 + 43.2 = **359.2 per skull** [R127].

| Skull | Repeat run | First run (× 3) |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | 359.2 | ≈ 1,078 |
| R4 | 1,437 | ≈ 4,310 |
| R6 | 2,155 | ≈ 6,466 |
| R10 | 3,592 | ≈ 10,776 |

- First runs at R4, R6 and R10 are in the same range as Axel's figures (about 4,500, 5,000 to 6,000 and about 10,000) [AX]. These are before Conquest and boosts. Most pages predate U81, and the U81.4 notes cite no Reaper XP change [R155].
- After that, the same modifiers as normal XP apply, except delving bonus and Tome of Learning (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220].
- Over-level penalty: heroic only, -10% when the highest-level character is 4 levels above the quest's base level [W-RD].

</details>

### 8.2 Which skull

- Run R4 at minimum at the cap, in a party. Solo is fine at R1 while leveling [AX].
- Use even skulls (R4, R6, R8, R10), since the big difficulty jumps fall at R5 and R7 [AX] [R152].
- Judge a skull by RXP per minute. If going up a skull doubles the duration, stay below it [AX].
- Expect Doom from R5 [UNVERIFIED].

### 8.3 Points

- Spend order: Grim Barricade first [AX] [W-RE].
- Target: 40 to 50 points (1.6M to 2.5M RXP) before the Racial lives [AX].
- The Nth point costs 2,000 N - 1,000 RXP, so N points = N² × 1,000 RXP [W-RE].

| Points | Cumulative RXP | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 25,000 | Grim Barricade Core 1 + Tier 1 [W-RE] |
| 25 | 625,000 | Reaper vendor 1: Raven Queen Messenger, cosmetic eyes, 1M pp (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220] |
| 50 | 2,500,000 | Reaper vendor 2: Augur, with Reaperling and Reaper Forge (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220] |

<details><summary>More on points</summary>

- 75 points (5,625,000 RXP): Reaper vendor 3, Herald, wings, 2M pp (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220]. The eyes and wings last one life and must be bought again after a True Reincarnation [R220].
- 1 capstone, or a full Dire Thaumaturge = 41 points (1,681,000 RXP). Three full trees (Dread 51, Dire 41, Grim 55) = 147 points (21,609,000 RXP) [W-RE].
- Tiers unlock by character level: Tier 1 at 4, Tier 2 at 10, Tier 3 at 17, Tier 4 at 21, Tier 5 at 27. Cores 1 to 6 at levels 4 / 9 / 15 / 21 / 25 / 30 [W-RE]. Cores 2 to 6 also work outside Reaper [W-RE].
- 40 points take about 250 first runs of a level 36 Long quest at R6 with no boosts (1,600,000 / 6,466), or about 150 with Conquest (+25%) and a +30% potion. One life does not offer that many new Legendary quests, so this is a total across several lives (calculation on [R127] [W-RE]).

</details>
