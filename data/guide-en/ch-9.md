## 9. The reincarnation loop

At the end of each 1 to 36 life (§10), pick your next reincarnation. This guide follows the cycle of Axel (the YouTube channel "Axel's DDO Channel") [AX]. The other orders in the closed blocks are player opinions.

### 9.1 Axel's cycle: one class life, one iconic, one epic per turn [AX]

1. **Enter an iconic life** with an Iconic True Heart of Wood (1,495 DDO Points or 4,200 Commendations, prices in §6.3) [R167]. You start at level 15 [R167]. Entering from a non-iconic life with this heart is not confirmed [UNVERIFIED].
2. **Level 15 to 20** with the route in §5, then 20 to 30. Fill the epic sagas without turning them in, and pick up Tokens and Commendations [AX].
3. **At level 30, jump to the cap** (§7). Run the life's Legendary quests in Reaper: the first run gives +200% Reaper XP (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-RD] [R220].
4. **Epic Reincarnation** (1,295 DDO Points or 4,200 Commendations) [R168]. You restart at level 20 [UNVERIFIED]. Drink the potion, turn in the sagas (§6.2) because a True Reincarnation follows, climb back up and finish the remaining Legendary quests.
5. **Iconic True Reincarnation (TR)**: you get the iconic past life and the past life of your dominant class. Start over [R218].

- One epic life between two iconics is enough without redoing quests. Two if you run a lot of slayers [AX].
- One turn gives 1 class past life, 1 iconic, 1 epic. That pays for a Heroic True Heart with Tokens [R165] [R166], the next Iconic Heart with Commendations [R167] and hundreds of thousands of Reaper XP. The real Commendation flow is not established (§11) [AX].
- **Mix your goals** in the same cycle: class life, iconic life, Epic Past Life, Reaper XP and Tokens [AX].
- **Choose the class of each iconic life** to work toward the Heroic Completionist (one past life in each of the 15 classes: +2 to all abilities and skills). The class that is dominant at level 30 gives the past life [AX].

### 9.2 Which stances to stack first [AX]

| Main style | Epic Past Life to put at 3 | Iconic Past Life to put at 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | Past Life: Martial Sphere: Doublestrike (+3% per stack) | Past Life: Razorclaw Shifter or Past Life: Aasimar Scourge |
| Arcane caster | Past Life: Arcane Sphere: Energy Criticals | Past Life: Eladrin Chaosmancer (general caster) |
| Ranged | Past Life: Primal Sphere: Doubleshot | none |
| Necro | none | Past Life: Dark Bargainer |
| Everyone | none | Past Life: Purple Dragon Knight (speed Action Boost), Past Life: Eladrin Chaosmancer (Fey Step), Past Life: Tiefling Scoundrel (+30% speed at 3 stacks, does not stack with Striding or with an upgraded Quiver of Alacrity) |

The Quiver of Alacrity (+10% ranged attack speed, heroic raid Ascension Chamber, level 17) is a key item for reincarnation lives [AX] [C-QA]. The Epic Completionist requires any 12 Epic Past Lives, not one of each [R193].

### 9.3 When to start the racial lives [AX]

Start when you have all of these: the Heroic Completionist, about two dozen Epic Past Lives, one iconic stance and one epic stance at 3 stacks, 40-50 Reaper Points, and a reserve of Tokens of the Twelve (20 per heart, §6.3). Racial lives give one past life per life, cover levels 1 to 20 only, and give no epic content and so no Tokens. The strong reward (+1 racial AP) only comes with the 3rd life of a race [AX].
