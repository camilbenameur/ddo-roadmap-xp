## 10. A reincarnated life, levels 1 to 36

Follow these 6 steps in order on every life. After an Epic Reincarnation, restart at step 3. An Iconic life starts at level 15 of step 2 (1,050,000 XP banked, wiki) [W-IH] [R218].

| Step | Levels | Do this | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reincarnation | Take a Heroic or Racial TR to level 1, or an Epic Reincarnation to level 20 [UNVERIFIED]. Pick the life's class for the past life you want. Buy a Heroic True Heart of Wood or of Blood with 20 Tokens of the Twelve. Use the 34-point buy on your first TR and 36 after that [WR]. | §3, §6.3, §9.1 |
| 2 | 1 to 20 | Play each quest once, on Elite or R1 right away. A character reincarnated twice unlocks Elite and Reaper without VIP (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220]. Level 20 costs 1,900,000 XP on lives 2 to 4, 2,850,000 on lives 5 to 7 and 3,800,000 from life 8 [N72] [R205]. | §2.3, §3.2, §4, §5 |
| 3 | 20 to 30 | Run the Epic sagas, and earn Tokens of the Twelve and Commendations. Count 8,250,000 XP, the same every life (a 2015 figure, unverified after U81) [R111] [R219]. Keep the Heroic sagas unturned only if a Lesser or Epic Reincarnation follows. Before a True or Racial Reincarnation, turn them in [W-SG]. | §3.2, §6, §6.2 |
| 4 | 30 to 36 | Instant if this character has already earned its Legendary XP. Otherwise earn 12,600,000 Legendary XP, once. It is kept on every life (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-LL]. | §7 |
| 5 | At the cap | Run the life's Legendary quests on Reaper, first run of each, for +200% Reaper XP (wiki, not cross-checked) [R220]. Pick your skull by Reaper XP per minute. Reaper Points carry over to the next life [W-RD]. | §8 |
| 6 | Next TR | Do not True Reincarnate from the cap before the life's Legendary quests are done (golden rule 8). Keep 20 Tokens for the next heart. | §1, §9 |

Sagas are lost on a True Reincarnation, so turn them in before it.

With little content past level 20, Axel's DDO Channel (YouTube) allows class lives at 1 to 20 with a Heroic TR [AX]. This Heroic TR is not available during an Iconic life (wiki, not cross-checked) [R218].
