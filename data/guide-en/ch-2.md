<details><summary>Tomes of Learning and power-leveling</summary>

Values from ddowiki, not cross-checked [W-XP] [W-LL].

| Tome | First-time bonus | Bonus on all XP |
|---|---|---|
| Lesser (heroic) | +25% heroic, +15% epic | +10% |
| Greater (heroic) | +50% heroic, +25% epic | +20% |
| Epic Lesser / Greater | | +5% / +10% |

- Tomes apply to quests and challenges only. They do nothing for XP stones (sagas, Daily Dice, Otto's Box), slayers, the monster manual, Reaper XP or Commendations [R214] [R215].
- The heroic Tome counts at levels 1-19. The Epic Tome counts from level 20 and stops when you take level 30. It follows the character's level, not the quest's. A legendary character who gains epic XP keeps it, a level 30 character does not [R214]. The Epic Tome does not apply to legendary XP.
- Lesser then Greater: the effect is upgraded and does not add up. A heroic Tome and an Epic Tome stack, each in its own range, and with all other XP bonuses. Tomes persist through True Reincarnation [R214].
- Power-leveling penalty: 50% XP at 4 levels below a party member, 75% at 5, 87.5% at 6. It does not affect epic and legendary characters [W-XP].

</details>

### 2.4 Elixirs

- The best elixir wins: Sovereign gives +50% for 3 hours (I) or 6 hours (II).
- The timer runs only inside a quest, while you are logged in. It pauses in public areas [R169] [R170] [R213].
- You can drink several. The strongest is active first and they do not add up (wiki, not cross-checked) [R213].

### 2.5 Equipped XP items and airship shrines

Each item gives +5% quest XP [R185].

| Item | Where to get it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Voice of the Master | end of Return to Delera's Tomb (heroic); level 5+ trinket | [R179] [C-VM] |
| Epic Voice of the Master | end of Return to Delera's Tomb (Epic); Pick of the Dragon's Lair gives one too | [R182] |
| Pale Green Ioun Stone | loot from the final chest of Shadow of a Doubt | [R180] |
| Mantle of the Worldshaper / Legendary Dream of the Worldshaper | Mantle: reward from The Abandoned Excavation. Dream: craft only, from the Mantle | [R181] [R183] |

Guild airship shrines give +1 to +5% XP [R186].

Stacking: only one equipped XP item counts, since each page says "does not stack with other equippable XP item effects" [R179] [R180] [R181] [R182] [R183] [R184]. This has not been tested in game. An elixir and VIP stack with it. The shrine stacks with elixirs, but shrine pages I and V say it does not stack with other equippable XP bonuses [R186]. So the best case is one 5% bonus from an item or a shrine, one elixir and VIP. Inferred: shrine V adds nothing to an equipped Voice of the Master [R186] [R179].
