### 6.2 The saga bank

1. Fill the Epic sagas on Elite or Reaper. Reaper counts as Elite for sagas.
2. Do not turn them in. Saga progress is kept on Lesser and Epic Reincarnation and lost on True and Racial (wiki, not cross-checked) [W-SG] [R217].
3. Take the Epic Reincarnation at 30. You return to level 20 (check the starting level in game, §3.1).
4. Drink your best elixir (§2.4) and wear one XP item (§2.5), then turn in all the sagas at once [AXS].
5. Take your levels between turn-ins, since the XP bank cap is 2 levels (exactly one level's XP at levels 20 and 30) [AXS]. Axel (the YouTube channel "Axel's DDO Channel") gets about 20 → 27 this way, skipping the weak low Epic levels [AX].

Heroic sagas follow the same rule: keep them unturned only if a Lesser or Epic Reincarnation follows. Before a True or Racial Reincarnation, turn them in.

**Saga rules** (wiki, not cross-checked) [R217]:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Points per quest | 1 on Casual or Normal, 2 on Hard, 3 on Elite or Reaper. Each quest counts once, at its highest difficulty before the turn-in |
| Reward tiers | Hard at 1.75 × the number of quests (rounded down), Elite at 2.5 ×, True Elite when all quests are on Elite |
| Catch-up | You can replay on Elite a quest done on Normal before turning in the saga |
| Shortcuts | A VIP can skip one quest (counted as Elite). Any player can skip one for 5 Astral Shards. 15 Astral Shards raise you one tier, once per saga and per turn-in |
| Cooldown | 100 hours on four Heroic sagas only: Pirates of the Thunder Sea (Heroic), Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh, Fables of the Feywild (Heroic), Isle of Dread (Heroic). It only blocks the next reward |

### 6.3 Tokens of the Twelve

<details><summary>Tokens of the Twelve and hearts</summary>

Play the quests below on Elite or Reaper, never on Casual (it never gives a token), up to 20 Tokens per heart. Only some Epic quests give them, in their end chest [AX] [C-TT] [R216]. Of 20 Tokens you buy a Heroic True Heart of Wood (class) or of Blood (racial) from Lahar, bound to the character (wiki, not cross-checked) [R216]. Free players: only the three Web of Chaos quests give them (The Lords of Dust, Servants of the Overlord, The Spinner of Shadows).

Quests that give Tokens [C-TT]: A Small Problem, Partycrashers, The Snitch, Under the Big Top, Gateway to Khyber, Haywire Foundry, Plane of Night, Tharashk Arena, The Jungle of Khyber, The Prisoner, The Vault of Night, Against the Demon Queen, An Offering of Blood, Chains of Flame, The Chamber of Raiyum, Zawabi's Revenge, Servants of the Overlord, The Lords of Dust, The Spinner of Shadows, Fathom the Depths, Into the Deep, The Claw of Vulkoor, The Last Stand, Bargain of Blood, Spies in the House, Storm the Beaches, The Black Loch, The Tide Turns, Devil Assault, The Chronoscope.

- 300 Epic Cannith ingredients = 1 Token from Kaldoth Maz (wiki, not cross-checked) [R216].

Drop rates, Epic Elite averages, rough and partly outdated (wiki, not cross-checked) [R216]: 1 token from Bargain of Blood, The Black Loch and The Tide Turns; 1.5 from Spies in the House, The Jungle of Khyber, Haywire Foundry and The Vault of Night; 2.5 from Chains of Flame. Calculation: 20 Tokens take at least 14 runs at 1.5 tokens per run.
